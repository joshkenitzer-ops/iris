Iris

Iris takes you from your existing resume to a tailored, verified application package. Here's what happens at each step.

  1. 0Starting Point
    You: Upload your resume, paste your career history, or start from scratch.
    Iris: Extracts your career history and organizes it by role and time period.
    Output: your career content, ready to audit.
  2. 1Audit
    You: Review findings and decide what to address.
    Iris: Runs an adversarial audit across five dimensions: content gaps, AI-sounding language, voice, formatting, and structure.
    Output: a prioritized list of findings before anything changes.
  3. 2Foundational Resume Build
    You: Provide missing context. Approve each section.
    Iris: Drafts bullets in bold-lead format. Locks every approved fact into your registry.
    Output: your foundational resume — the source of truth, not a document you send.
  4. 3–4Slop & Formatting Checks
    You: Nothing — findings surface automatically.
    Iris: Checks for AI-sounding language, banned vocabulary, em dashes, date formatting, and ATS compliance on every draft.
    Output: inline findings surfaced as you work.
  5. 5Fit Check
    You: Paste a job description. Decide whether to proceed.
    Iris: Maps the role's requirements against your registry. Surfaces strong matches and names real gaps honestly.
    Output: a fit summary. Iris never blocks you from applying — you decide.
  6. 6Tailoring
    You: Review and approve the tailored resume.
    Iris: Reorders and reframes your strongest matching facts. Rewrites your headline and summary for the role. Adds nothing that isn't already in your registry.
    Output: a tailored resume draft.
  7. 7Cover Letter
    You: Review the gap language. Approve the closing.
    Iris: Writes a paired cover letter built on your strongest matched requirements. Names real gaps directly — never hides them.
    Output: a cover letter matched to the tailored resume.
  8. 8Final Review
    You: Review findings. Approve the final package.
    Iris: Runs a three-tier programmatic check: critical claims, full slop scan, and a final read as an experienced hiring reviewer.
    Output: your final resume and cover letter, ready to download and submit.
Iris Profile — at the end of a session, download your Iris Profile. Upload it at the start of your next session and Iris will remember everything: your locked facts, dismissed findings, and package history. Without it, you start from scratch.

Iris is early. Your feedback directly shapes what gets built next. Takes about 3 minutes.
